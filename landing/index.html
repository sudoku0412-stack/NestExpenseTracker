FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is NestExpenseTracker free?

Yes. Download it free on iOS and Android. Premium adds extras for people who want more.

Where is my data stored?

On your device first. If you join a household, receipts sync to that household only. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can my partner or family use it too?

Yes. Invite them to your household and you share receipts, budgets and splits.

Does it work in other languages?

The app is available in English and French, and handles many currencies.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add receipts and entries yourself, so nothing is linked to your bank accounts.
